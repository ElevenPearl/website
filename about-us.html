About Eleven Pearl

A Surat lighting company, established in 2003.

Our story

Built on light.
Driven by detail.

Eleven Pearl began in Surat in 2003, founded by Mr. Jeevan Mehta. What started as a local lighting business has grown through long-term relationships with designers, retailers and project teams.

We assemble and customise lighting systems for residences, hospitality, retail and commercial spaces. The work is practical: understand the brief, recommend useful products and make sure they perform well on site.

We stay involved from the first conversation to after-sales support. That continuity is why customers return to us, project after project.
