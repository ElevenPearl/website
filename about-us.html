About Eleven Pearl

Architectural light, shaped with purpose.

Eleven Pearl is a Surat-based architectural lighting company with more than two decades of industry experience. From concept to supply, we help create lighting schemes that feel as good as they perform.

Our story

Built on light.
Driven by detail.

Established in 2003 by Mr. Jeevan Mehta, Eleven Pearl has earned a strong reputation in architectural and decorative lighting through its commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction. We assemble, customise, distribute and supply premium lighting solutions for projects across India.

Our experienced team of technicians, production personnel, sales professionals and administrative staff combines modern lighting technology with contemporary design—delivering solutions that improve functionality, aesthetics and energy efficiency.

Whether supporting a home, retail destination, hospitality setting or large commercial project, we work closely with architects, interior designers, builders and homeowners to find the right balance of ambience, performance and practical detail.
